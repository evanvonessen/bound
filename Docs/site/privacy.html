Local gameplay and notes

You can play without a Bound account. Imported games, native saves, save states, controller settings and per-game Bound notes are kept in the app’s local storage. Bound’s per-game notes are not connected to its friend-account service or cloud notes sync in this implementation.

If you choose an export or sharing destination, that destination receives the file you select. Device backups and any separately enabled sync service are governed by your settings and that service.

Bound accounts

When you sign in, your email address and password are sent to the existing Bound authentication service hosted by Supabase. Bound stores the resulting session and refresh credentials in the device’s Keychain so it can restore sign-in and renew an expiring session. The app does not retain your password.

Account identifiers, friend display names, invitations, friend connections and room/session information support the friend feature. Signing out clears the saved local session and invalidates the app’s current credential holders. It does not delete the remote account.

Friend game video

Sharing begins when you start a session. Bound sends captured game pixels through Agora so your selected friend can view your gameplay. Your friend may see personal information that appears inside the game. The current sharing path does not send the app interface, local notes or game files, and does not publish camera, microphone or game-audio streams.

Stopping sharing, signing out, returning to Library, changing games or backgrounding the app stops this session. A friend can still capture what they see on their own device. Bound does not request cloud recording through this sharing implementation.

Supabase handles authentication and friend/token requests; Agora handles the video connection. These services receive the information needed to operate those features, including account or channel identifiers and network request information. Their processing and retention depend on their policies and the applicable service configuration; no fixed retention period is promised here.

See Supabase’s privacy policy and Agora’s privacy policy.

Optional library sync and external content

The Delta foundation retains optional Google Drive and Dropbox synchronization. This is separate from the Bound friend account. If you connect and enable it, the sync implementation can transfer game files, artwork, native saves, save-state thumbnails, controller skins and related library records to your selected service. It can also include required system files for supported systems. Do not enable sync for files you do not want transferred.

Loading artwork from an external URL contacts that host, which receives ordinary network request information. Visiting external links, connecting optional accounts or using other upstream services is subject to the relevant provider’s practices. The app does not make a blanket claim that all information stays on the device.

See Google’s privacy policy and Dropbox’s privacy policy.

Retention, deletion and your choices

  • Play locally without signing into a Bound account.
  • Choose whether to connect a friend and when to stop sharing.
  • Choose whether to connect an optional library sync service.
  • Use the app’s local file controls and your storage provider’s controls to manage local or synced copies.

Signing out or uninstalling the app does not by itself delete account, friend or separately synced information held by service providers. Exported files and copies on other devices may remain.

Contact developer@evanvonessen.com to request access, correction or deletion of information associated with Bound’s account service. We may need to verify that the request relates to your account. The current version does not provide the older Bound app’s in-app account-deletion workflow. Available rights depend on where you live.

Retention depends on the service and how it is configured. Service backups, operational logs and support correspondence may remain according to provider practices and applicable requirements.

Support messages and this website

Bound is operated by Evan Von Essen. If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments you provide are used to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Send only what is needed; do not send passwords, tokens, game files or private save files.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its page code uses no analytics, advertising scripts, external fonts, forms or cookies. GitHub may process visitor and request information as the host. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. External links open services with their own policies.

Reports and blocks

In builds with reporting and blocking, Bound stores account and room identifiers, a report reason category, submission/review status and times, and your block choices with a friend display label. Reports are available to the Bound operator in a private review queue, not to other app users. Reporting does not automatically capture or upload video, audio, screenshots, private notes, or game files. Learn more in the community rules and moderation policy.

Account deletion removes the new report/block records associated with that account. Minimal deletion markers and hashed retry capabilities remain to reject stale sessions and resolve deletion retries. Separate provider data and local game files are managed separately. These new controls require the upcoming app update and backend rollout.
