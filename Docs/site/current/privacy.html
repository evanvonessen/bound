Bound privacy policy

Updated October 20, 2026.

Bound is operated by Evan Von Essen. Privacy requests: developer@evanvonessen.com.

Games, saves, settings and notes are stored on your device. You can play without an account. Exporting or enabling sync sends the selected files to that destination.

For accounts and friend features, Supabase processes your email, password, username, account identifiers, friend connections and session information. Session tokens are stored in your device’s Keychain.

Sharing sends game video through Agora to your friend, who may capture it. Camera and microphone streams are not sent. The services receive connection identifiers and network information to run these features.

If you enable Jiggle notifications, Bound stores your installation identifier and push token. Apple delivers the notifications. Reports and blocks store account and room identifiers, reasons, status and times for moderation; reports do not attach gameplay recordings.

Optional Dropbox sync uploads games, saves, artwork and library files. Previously connected RetroAchievements and Patreon accounts may still exchange account data; RetroAchievements also receives gameplay activity. Artwork requests send network information to the image host.

You can stop sharing, sign out, turn off notifications or disconnect sync. These actions do not delete stored account data. Request deletion in Settings, Account & Sync, Delete account. If this fails or is unavailable, email the address above.

Bound account data is kept until account deletion. A minimal deletion record remains to prevent old sessions from restoring access. Delete local files and Dropbox copies separately. Provider logs and backups may remain under their retention policies. Support emails are used to answer requests and investigate problems.

Services receiving your data must provide at least the protection described here. Their policies: Supabase, Agora, Dropbox, Apple, RetroAchievements and Patreon.

This site uses no cookies or analytics scripts. GitHub hosts it and receives visitor network information.
