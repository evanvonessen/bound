Bound community rules

No harassment, hate, threats, sexual content, graphic violence, scams or illegal material. Don’t share someone’s private information or content you don’t have permission to use.

In Friends, open the menu beside a friend to report or block them. You can stop sharing at any time.

Evan Von Essen reviews reports and may restrict accounts. For help or to appeal a restriction, email developer@evanvonessen.com.
