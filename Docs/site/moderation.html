The rules

These rules apply to friend display names, invitations, and shared gameplay. Do not use Bound to:

  • Harass, threaten, stalk, or target people with hate or discrimination.
  • Share sexual exploitation material, sexual content involving minors, or other illegal content.
  • Share explicit sexual content, graphic violence, threats of harm, or another person’s private information.
  • Impersonate others, send spam or scams, evade blocks or suspensions, or abuse the reporting tools.
  • Share games or other content you do not have permission to use.

Only connect with people you want to share with. Stop sharing whenever you feel uncomfortable.

Report and block

In versions with these controls, open Friends and the actions beside a friend. Choose Report and a reason to send a report, or Block to stop that connection and prevent either account from connecting again while the block is in place. Manage your own blocks under Blocked → Unblock. Unblocking does not restore an old connection; exchange a new invite if you both wish to reconnect.

Blocking stops sharing with that friend on your device immediately. The service checks blocks and suspensions before issuing or renewing sharing tokens. An already issued token cannot be withdrawn instantly by these controls, so interruption on the other device can take longer.

A report contains the reporting account identifier, the reported account identifier, the friend-room identifier, a reason category, and submission/review status and times. Reporting does not automatically capture or upload gameplay video, audio, screenshots, private notes, or game files. Do not send passwords or sign-in tokens.

Report and block are being added in an upcoming app update. If your build does not have them, stop sharing and contact support below. The policy does not announce App Store availability.

Review and enforcement

Reports go to a private review queue for the Bound operator. The operator reviews available report information and can dismiss a report or suspend an account’s friend invitations and sharing access. A suspension can be lifted following review. Reports are not proof of wrongdoing by themselves.

Display-name checks reject some offensive terms, contact/link text, and control characters; unsafe older labels can be hidden. These limited text checks do not examine all live gameplay video or guarantee that all objectionable content is detected. Reporting and blocking remain available for concerns the filter misses.

No guaranteed response time or continuous monitoring is promised. This is not an emergency service. For an immediate threat, stop sharing and contact appropriate local emergency services.
